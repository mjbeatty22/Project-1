# Project 1 — Edition A: Walmart Inc. (WMT)

**Prepared:** September 1, 2026  
**Decision user:** Fund Investment Committee  
**Valuation date:** September 1, 2026  
**Status:** Screen-grade; not an authorization to buy or sell

## Decision and initial thesis

**Decision requested:** Approve a focused research underwrite of Walmart Inc. before any initiation decision.

**Initial stance: WATCH / DEFER.** Walmart's fiscal-2026 filing shows a very large, growing retail platform with positive operating cash generation and meaningful liquidity. It does *not*, by itself, establish whether WMT's share price offers an attractive expected return. The current share price, market capitalization, consensus expectations, peer valuation, and a forward cash-flow/earnings model have not yet been assembled. The appropriate initial action is to watch and complete the valuation and expectation work rather than initiate a position.

## Consequential assumptions

1. The 8,022 million weighted-average diluted shares are a reasonable *screening* input for a preliminary equity-value bridge; a full valuation must reconcile current basic shares outstanding, options/RSUs, and any other dilutive securities as of the valuation date.
2. The $44,762 million debt figure is treated as debt for a first-pass enterprise-value bridge and excludes finance-lease obligations. Whether leases, nonredeemable noncontrolling interests, pensions, and other claims belong in enterprise value must be decided consistently with the selected valuation method.
3. The fiscal-2026 reported revenue and operating-income trajectory is informative, but it is not a forecast. No forward sales growth, margin, tax rate, capital-expenditure, working-capital, or terminal-value assumption has been adopted.
4. September 1, 2026 is the valuation date for this Edition A. No closing WMT share price, market capitalization, or valuation multiple has been sourced yet; therefore no equity value, enterprise value, target price, or upside/downside is asserted.

## Unknowns that matter to the decision

- WMT closing price, market capitalization, trading liquidity, and enterprise value at September 1, 2026.
- Consensus revenue, EPS, EBITDA, and free-cash-flow estimates; the market's embedded growth and margin expectations.
- The durability and economics of higher-margin businesses (advertising, membership, marketplace, fulfillment, healthcare, and financial services) versus the retail core.
- Segment-level growth, margin, returns on incremental capital, e-commerce profitability, and competitive position.
- Capital-allocation priorities, future debt/lease obligations, buybacks, dividends, and dilution.
- The downside mechanism: pressure on price, mix, wage/fulfillment costs, international results, consumer demand, or valuation multiple — and the sensitivity of equity value to each.
- Valuation support from a forward model, comparable companies, and scenario analysis.

## Research plan

1. **Establish the valuation baseline:** obtain WMT's September 1, 2026 closing price and shares outstanding; calculate market capitalization and an explicit enterprise-value bridge including debt, cash, and noncontrolling interests.
2. **Build the operating model:** normalize fiscal 2024–2026 income statement, cash flow, balance sheet, and segment data; forecast revenue, gross margin, operating expense, capex, and working capital for at least three years.
3. **Test business quality:** break down Walmart U.S., Sam's Club, and International performance; identify the contribution, growth, margin, and capital intensity of advertising, membership, marketplace, and fulfillment.
4. **Frame expectations and valuation:** compare the forecast with consensus and historical ranges; evaluate P/E, EV/EBIT, EV/EBITDA, and a cash-flow-based valuation using explicit assumptions and a base/bull/bear framework.
5. **Underwrite downside and falsifiers:** quantify earnings and valuation sensitivity to sales growth, margin, capital intensity, and multiple compression; define observable triggers and the evidence needed to change the watch/defer stance.
6. **Return to the IC:** recommend initiate, continue to watch, or do not initiate only after the valuation, expectation, and downside work supports a stated expected-return range.
